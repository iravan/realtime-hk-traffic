# Realtime HK Traffic — Feature List

For everyone getting around Hong Kong.

## Live arrivals, in one tap
- Next MTR train on every line that serves a station, with service status.
- Light Rail (輕鐵) arrivals.
- KMB, LWB, Citybus and New Lantao Bus arrivals by route and stop.
- Green minibus ETAs where an official feed exists.
- Hong Kong & Kowloon Ferry and Sun Ferry departures with clock times.
- Taxi stand locations.

## A map that stays fast
- All stops drawn as pins on an OpenStreetMap basemap; no sign-in needed.
- Pans and zooms without asking the server for stops.
- Counts group into bubbles at low zoom; tap to zoom in.

## Stop details
- Station or stop name in English or Chinese.
- Serving routes shown as coloured chips.
- MTR facilities: toilet, water dispenser, babycare room, Wi-Fi.
- Ferry timetables and destinations.
- Service hours where published.

## Built for Hong Kong
- Bilingual (English / 繁體中文) interface.
- Location-aware start: opens your nearest stop.
- Each tab remembers the last stop you opened.
- Shareable stop links.
- Works as a home-screen app on iOS and Android (PWA).
- No ads, no account, free to use.
